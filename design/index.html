Design & analysis

Turn a fold into an engineered flexure.

Material properties, geometry, and loading assumptions will be tracked from the first concept through the final computational model.

Material screening

Select for deformation, durability, and manufacture.

Candidate materials will be compared using the same documented criteria. Published properties will remain separate from values measured by the team.

PropertyWhy it mattersPlanned evidence
Elastic modulusInfluences hinge stiffness and the force required to foldManufacturer data, literature, and model input
Strength and strain capacityLimits the allowable deformation before damage or failurePublished properties and specimen testing where available
Fatigue behaviorDetermines the hinge's ability to survive repeated foldingCycles to damage, stiffness change, and failure mode
PrintabilityAffects dimensional consistency, defects, and repeatabilityPrint settings, visual inspection, and dimensional checks
Availability and costConstrains the practical prototype programSupplier availability and per-specimen material estimate

Computational analysis

A reproducible model, not just a color contour.

Every result published here will include enough context to understand how it was generated.

Model definition
  • CAD geometry and relevant dimensions
  • Material model and property sources
  • Element type and mesh-refinement study
  • Contacts, constraints, and panel interfaces
  • Large-deformation assumptions where applicable
Load cases and outputs
  • Prescribed folding angles or applied moments
  • Peak stress and strain locations
  • Force or moment versus rotation
  • Permanent deformation or instability indicators
  • Strength in both bistable configurations
