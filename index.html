Project at a glance

Compact when stored. Strong when deployed.

The project connects origami-inspired geometry with compliant-mechanism design and additive manufacturing.

Challenge

Limited launch volume

Space structures must be lightweight and compact while retaining useful structural capability after deployment.

Approach

Printed flexure joints

Candidate materials and hinge geometries will be modeled, printed, cycled, and compared under controlled loading.

Outcome

Evidence-based selection

The final recommendation will connect material choice and hinge thickness to foldability, fatigue, and bistable strength.

Objectives

Four questions guide the work.

01

Which materials?

Find 3D-printable materials suitable for compliant mechanisms.

02

Which geometry?

Design a compliant hinge for use in thick-panel origami structures.

03

How will it behave?

Model the hinge and perform computational analysis.

04

How well will it perform?

Measure fatigue, folding angle, thickness effects, and strength in bistable positions.
