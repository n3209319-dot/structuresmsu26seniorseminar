Planned phases

The project from question to recommendation.

01

Research and requirements

Review compliant mechanisms, thick-panel origami, printable materials, and relevant performance measures.

Output: screening criteria

02

Material down-selection

Compare candidate materials and identify practical specimens for initial testing.

Output: material matrix

03

Concept and CAD development

Create alternative hinge geometries and parameterize the key dimensions.

Output: concept decision

04

Computational analysis

Evaluate folding response, stress, strain, and sensitivity to thickness.

Output: model baseline

05

Prototype manufacturing

Print traceable specimen sets and document settings, dimensions, and defects.

Output: test specimens

06

Experimental characterization

Measure fold range, fatigue, thickness effects, and strength in bistable positions.

Output: verified data

07

Optimization and validation

Compare simulation and experiment, select the best configuration, and state limitations.

Output: final recommendation
