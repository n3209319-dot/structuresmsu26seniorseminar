Testing & results

Measure the hinge through every fold.

The experimental program will connect material and thickness to fold angle, fatigue, failure, and strength in both stable configurations.

Experimental plan

Controlled variables. Traceable specimens.

Each specimen will be labeled and linked to its material, geometry, print parameters, conditioning, test method, and observed failure mode.

01

Manufacture

Print controlled specimen sets using documented settings and orientations.

02

Inspect

Record dimensions, visible defects, and specimen condition before loading.

03

Load

Apply controlled rotation, cyclic motion, or stable-position loads.

04

Compare

Relate measurements to material choice, hinge thickness, and model predictions.
