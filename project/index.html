Project overview

The engineering problem behind the fold.

A clear statement of the need, the research question, and the evidence the team plans to produce.

Problem statement

Deployable structures must save space without surrendering function.

With growing interest in space travel, lightweight and compact structures are needed for a wide range of missions. Compliant mechanisms and origami-inspired geometries can create load-bearing, bistable folding structures that occupy less storage volume while preserving useful structural capability.

This project will identify suitable 3D-printable materials, develop a compliant hinge for thick-panel origami, and evaluate its performance through computational analysis and controlled stress testing. The central performance variables are folding angle, fatigue life, hinge thickness, and strength in each bistable position.

How do material selection and hinge thickness affect the foldability, fatigue life, and load-bearing performance of a printed compliant hinge?

Working vocabulary

Three ideas, one mechanism.

Thick-panel origami

Folding with real thickness

Origami-inspired motion applied to structural panels whose thickness affects geometry and movement.

Compliant mechanism

Motion through deformation

A mechanism that moves through elastic flexing rather than conventional pins, bearings, or assembled joints.

Bistability

Two stable configurations

The ability of a structure to remain in either of two positions without continuous external force.

Measures of success

Performance will be judged with repeatable evidence.

Numerical acceptance thresholds will be finalized with the faculty advisors after the baseline material and design studies.

Printability

Repeatable specimens

Consistent geometry, acceptable defects, and documented print parameters.

Foldability

Useful angular range

Controlled rotation without unacceptable permanent deformation or failure.

Durability

Fatigue resistance

Measured cycles, stiffness change, crack initiation, and failure mode.

Strength

Bistable capacity

Load-bearing or holding performance in both stable configurations.

Correlation

Model agreement

Comparison between predicted and experimentally observed behavior.

Traceability

Documented decisions

Clear links among requirements, analysis, testing, and the final recommendation.
